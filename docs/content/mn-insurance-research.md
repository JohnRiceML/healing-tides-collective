# Research: therapy + insurance in Minnesota

**Researched 2026-07-29.** Source material for the journal's MN-payer series — the demand map's
#1 content gap (every payer SERP is templated listings; nobody *explains* how MN coverage works).

> **This is a fact base, not copy.** Everything is tagged VERIFIED (primary source, URL) or
> UNCERTAIN. Nothing tagged UNCERTAIN may be published, and the "Confirm before publishing" list
> at the bottom is a hard gate — several items are plan-year-sensitive or litigation-dependent.

## The two findings to lead with

Both are MN-specific, statute-verifiable, and almost nobody has written them up for a general
audience. They're the reason this series can outrank national templates.

1. **Prior authorization for outpatient mental health is banned in Minnesota** — Minn. Stat.
   [62M.07](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/62M.07), for plans issued/renewed on or after
   **2026-01-01** (medications excepted). VERIFIED from statute text.
2. **The network-gap right** — Minn. Stat. [62Q.58 subd. 4](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/62Q.58):
   when a plan has no appropriate participating specialist reasonably available, it **must** refer
   to a nonparticipating one, and "services must be provided at no additional cost to the enrollee
   beyond what the enrollee would otherwise pay" in network. VERIFIED from statute text. This is the
   single most useful under-known right for a Minnesotan who can't find an in-network therapist.

Supporting: **Minn. Stat. [62Q.47](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/62Q.47)** — therapy
visits "shall be considered primary care visits" for cost-sharing, so therapy should hit the
*primary-care* copay tier, not specialist.

## Public programs

- **MA cost-sharing is $0 by statute** — Minn. Stat. [256B.0631](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/256B.0631). VERIFIED
- **⚠ Eligibility resets each 7/1.** Adults 19–64 at 133% FPG = $1,768/mo (HH of 1), 7/1/26–6/30/27;
  no asset test. VERIFIED — DHS-3461A
- **MinnesotaCare** ≤200% FPG; "no cost sharing for mental health services." VERIFIED —
  [DHS EPM 3.1.2.2](https://hcopub.dhs.state.mn.us/epm/3_1_2_2.htm). Premium/copay dollar figures
  **UNCERTAIN** (DHS-4139A bot-blocked) — do not publish
- Diagnostic assessment required before ongoing psychotherapy; up to **3 sessions** may precede it. VERIFIED
- **Directory:** [MHCP Provider Directory](https://mhcpproviderdirectory.dhs.state.mn.us/) — **fee-for-service
  members only**; managed-care members must use their MCO's directory. DHS advises calling to confirm
- **"Few therapists take MA," sourced honestly:** DHS CY2022 mean paid — 90837 (60-min) **$120 FFS /
  $116 MCO**; 90834 (45-min) **$96 / $96**. VERIFIED — [DHS rates report Table 7 p.16](https://www.lrl.mn.gov/docs/2024/mandated/240362.pdf).
  Against $215–$245 self-pay, that's the whole story. **⚠ 2025 law (SF1402) raised RBRVS professional
  rates to ≥100% of Medicare effective 2026-01-01** — confirm current numbers before citing CY2022
- **Never assert a "% of MN therapists who accept MA"** — no source quantifies it. UNVERIFIABLE

## Out-of-network / superbills

- **Superbill must carry:** provider name/credentials/NPI/EIN, address, client name/DOB, ICD-10 dx,
  CPT (90791 intake / 90834 45-min / 90837 60-min) + telehealth modifier, date & place of service,
  amount paid, signature. VERIFIED
- Reimbursement is a % of the plan's **allowed amount, not what you paid**, after a **separate OON
  deductible**. Mechanism VERIFIED; specific percentages UNCERTAIN — never publish one
- **MN reality check:** most MNsure plans are HMOs with no OON benefit at all, so for many readers
  the superbill path doesn't exist. Say so plainly

**The phone script** (ask for "outpatient behavioral health," not "therapy"): OON outpatient mental
health benefits? OON deductible + how much met? Coinsurance % after? Allowed amount for **90837 and
90834**? Telehealth covered OON (POS 10 / modifier 95)? Visit limit? Prior auth (note: banned for
outpatient MH as of 2026-01-01)? Claim submission + timely-filing deadline? **And: if no in-network
therapist is available, will you approve a network-gap exception at in-network cost-sharing under
62Q.58?**

## Rights that matter

- **Federal MHPAEA** parity — financial requirements, quantitative limits, NQTLs. **⚠ The 2024 final
  rule is under a non-enforcement policy announced 2025-05-15** pending *ERIC v. DOL* + 18 months;
  the statute and 2013 rule remain enforceable. **Recheck at publish and every update.** VERIFIED
- **MN parity:** 62Q.47 (above) and [62Q.53](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/62Q.53)
  (no stricter "medically necessary" definition for mental health)
- **MN Commerce has enforced it** — HealthPartners **$150,000** (2023-05), Medica **$300,000**
  (2023-07), UnitedHealthcare **$450,000** (2024-05). All VERIFIED, all citable. Proof that appealing works
- **Where to complain:** fully-insured non-HMO → MN Commerce 800-657-3602 · HMO → MDH Managed Care
  800-657-3916 · self-funded employer plan → US DOL EBSA 1-866-444-3272
- **External review** ([62Q.73](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/62Q.73)): file within
  **6 months**, **binding on the plan but not on you**, **cost borne by the plan** — the $25 fee was
  eliminated 2024-08-01. **⚠ The MN AG handbook still says $25; it's stale — don't cite it**
- **Network adequacy:** Minn. Stat. **62K.10** (⚠ *not* 62K.14 — that's pediatric dental, a common
  error). MDH mental-health standard: lesser of **30 miles or 30 minutes**
- **No Surprises Act good-faith estimate:** uninsured/self-pay have a right to a written GFE within
  **3 business days** of scheduling; if billed **≥$400 over**, dispute within **120 days**. Note
  honestly that NSA balance-billing protections **don't** cover routine outpatient therapy
- **Telehealth** ([62A.673](https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/62A.673)): coverage + payment
  parity, cost-sharing no greater than in-person. **⚠ Audio-only counts as telehealth only until
  2027-07-01** — hard sunset, date the article
